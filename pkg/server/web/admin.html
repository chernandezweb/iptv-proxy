💡 How to get your NordVPN SOCKS5 Service Credentials

NordVPN SOCKS5 requires Service Credentials (not your login email). To find them:
  1. Log into your Nord Account Dashboard.
  2. Go to Services → NordVPN → Manual Setup (or Service Credentials).
  3. Copy your generated Username and Password, paste them above, and click 💾 Save & Apply Proxy.
